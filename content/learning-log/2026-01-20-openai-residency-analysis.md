## Context

Preparing for OpenAI Residency application - a 6-month program for brilliant technical researchers from diverse fields to transition into AI. Full salary, potential full-time offer after completion.

## Key Insights from Official Page

### What OpenAI Wants

| Phrase | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| "early builders doing meaningful work" | Ship public projects, not just learn |
| "move quickly from idea to prototype" | Show execution velocity |
| "strong research instincts" | Curiosity about WHY, not just HOW |
| "unusual self-direction" | Drive your own learning path |
| "bold yet methodical" | Think big, validate carefully |

### Critical Quote

> "We value excellence **regardless of institution or background**, and encourage applicants who are **self-taught**, **non-traditional**, or **early in their careers** if they have a clear record of **building and learning with intensity**."

### Success Stories

- **Will DePue**: No AI background → contributed from day one
- **Tyna Eloundou**: "Bold yet methodical"
- **Dan Mossing**: Biology → AI (they said YES!)

## Strategy

1. **Build in Public** - Every project ships with blog post
2. **Learning Journal** - Document daily progress (this series!)
3. **Ship Weekly** - Small but complete projects
4. **Research Instincts** - Read papers, question assumptions
